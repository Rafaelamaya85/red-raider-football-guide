# Michael Crabtree

Michael Crabtree is one of the most memorable and arguably the greatest wide receiver in Texas Tech football history. He played for the Red Raiders during the 2007 and 2008 seasons and became a major part of Tech's high powered offense.

## Career at Texas Tech

Crabtree rapidly established himself as one of the top receivers in college football. During his time at Texas Tech, he was known for his physical playing style, ability to make big catches and score touchdowns. He won the Biletnikoff Award, signifying the nation's top college receiver, in both 2007 and 2008.

## The Catch Against Texas

One of Crabtree's most famous moments and possibly the University's greatest moment came against Texas in 2008. With only seconds remaining in the game, Crabtree caught a pass from Graham Harrell near the sideline and scored the game winning touchdown.

The play gave Texas Tech a 39-33 victory over the consensus number one ranked team in the country , as well as bitter rival, and became one of the most famous moments in Red Raider football history.

## Legacy

Crabtree's success helped make the 2008 season one of the most memorable in Texas Tech history. After leaving Texas Tech, he was selected 10th overall in the first round of the 2009 NFL Draft by the San Francisco 49ers.
