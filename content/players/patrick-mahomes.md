# Patrick Mahomes

Patrick Mahomes is the most recognizable player to come out of the Texas Tech football program. He played quarterback for the Red Raiders from 2014 to 2016 before transitioning his career to the NFL.

## Texas Tech Career

Mahomes became the starting quarterback at Texas Tech during the 2015 season. He was the ideal quaterback for Texas Tech's high powered offense, primarily because of his strong arm, ability to make plays outside of the pocket, and big play ability.

During his time at Texas Tech, Mahomes produced some of the most memorable quarterback performances in program history.

## Record Setting Performance

One of Mahomes' most memorable games came against Oklahoma in 2016. In the game, Mahomes threw for 734 yards and five touchdowns.

The game became one of the most famous offensive performances in college football history, with Texas Tech and Oklahoma combining for 125 points.

## From Texas Tech to the NFL

After the 2016 season, Mahomes entered the NFL Draft where he was selected 10th overall by the Kansas City Chiefs.

His success in the NFL has made him arguably the best quaterback currently in the league which continues to bring attention to the Tech football program.
