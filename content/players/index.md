# Notable Texas Tech Players

Texas Tech has produced many memorable players throughout its football history. This section highlights three Red Raiders who made a major impact on the program and went on to become well known beyond Texas Tech.

## Graham Harrell

[[graham-harrell|Graham Harrell]] was the quarterback during one of the most successful periods of Texas Tech football. Playing under head coach Mike Leach, Harrell helped lead the Air Raid offense and was the quarterback during the memorable 2008 season.

## Michael Crabtree

[[michael-crabtree|Michael Crabtree]] became one of the most recognizable wide receivers in Texas Tech history. He was a major part of the Red Raiders' offense and caught the game winning touchdown against Texas in the final seconds of the famous 2008 game.

## Patrick Mahomes

[[patrick-mahomes|Patrick Mahomes]] played quarterback at Texas Tech before beginning his NFL career. His strong arm, athletic ability, and exciting style of play made him one of the most memorable quarterbacks to play for the Red Raiders.
