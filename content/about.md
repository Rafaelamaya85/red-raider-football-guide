## About Me

My name is Rafael Amaya, and I am a Senior at Texas Tech University. I am 40 years old and finishing a life long dream of obtaining my degree from Tech. I created the Red Raider Football Guide because not only am I a Texas Tech football fan  but I am a pretty die hard sports fan. I am constantly explaining the history of my teams to my wife and kids so naturally building a knowledge base that organizes all of the useful information about the program seemed like the best fit.

This site will include information about Texas Tech football history, players, coaches, traditions, Jones AT&T Stadium (currently Galaxy, but forever Jones) , and the game day experience in Lubbock. From the Pirate Mike Leach to Joey McGuire, we will try and cover it all. 

## About This Project

The Red Raider Football Guide was created as a knowledge base project using Markdown, Obsidian, GitHub, and GitHub Pages.
