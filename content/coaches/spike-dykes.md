# Spike Dykes

Spike Dykes was the head football coach at Texas Tech from 1986 through 1999. His length of career with the Red Raiders made him an important part of Texas Tech football history.

## Career at Texas Tech

Dykes took over the Texas Tech football program in 1986 and remained the head coach for more than a decade. During his time in Lubbock, the Red Raiders competed in the Southwest Conference and later the Big 12.

## Success

Dykes led Texas Tech to several winning seasons and bowl appearances. He also coached the Red Raiders during the program's move from the Southwest Conference to the Big 12.

His teams helped keep Texas Tech competitive during an important period of change in college football.

## Legacy

Dykes remains one of the longest tenured head coaches in Texas Tech football history. His time at Tech helped build the program before Mike Leach took over in 2000.
