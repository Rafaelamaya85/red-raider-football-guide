# Texas Tech Coaches

Texas Tech football has been shaped by many coaches throughout its history. This section highlights three coaches from different eras who each made an impact on the Red Raider football program.

## Joey McGuire

[[joey-mcguire|Joey McGuire]] is the current head coach of the Texas Tech Red Raiders. He is known for his energy, recruiting, and strong connections to Texas high school football. His time at Texas Tech represents the current era of the program.

## Mike Leach

[[mike-leach|Mike Leach]] coached Texas Tech from 2000 to 2009. His [[../football-history/air-raid-offense|Air Raid offense]] helped make Texas Tech one of the most exciting offensive teams in college football. His tenure included the memorable 2008 season and the famous win over Texas in Lubbock.

## Spike Dykes

[[spike-dykes|Spike Dykes]] was the head coach at Texas Tech from 1986 to 1999. He became an important part of Red Raider football history and helped establish the program before the Leach era. His long career and personality made him a well known figure among Texas Tech fans.
