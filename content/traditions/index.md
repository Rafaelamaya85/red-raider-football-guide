# Texas Tech Traditions

Texas Tech is known for traditions that make Red Raider football and game days unique. From the sights and sounds inside the stadium to some of the university's most recognizable symbols, these traditions are an important part of the Texas Tech experience.

## The Masked Rider

[[masked-rider|The Masked Rider]] is one of Texas Tech's most recognizable traditions. Riding onto the field on horseback, the Masked Rider has become a symbol of the university and an important part of Red Raider football game days.

## Raider Red

[[raider-red|Raider Red]] is Texas Tech's costumed mascot and another familiar face at athletic events. Raider Red helps bring energy and school spirit to football games and represents Texas Tech at events throughout the year.

## Goin' Band from Raiderland

[[goin-band-from-raiderland|The Goin' Band from Raiderland]] provides the soundtrack to Texas Tech football game days. From pregame performances to music in the stands, the band is an important part of the atmosphere surrounding Red Raider football.

## Traditions on Game Day

These traditions come together on football Saturdays in Lubbock. Fans can learn more about the full experience in the [[../game-day/game-day-traditions|Game Day Traditions]] page or use the [[../game-day/game-day-guide|Game Day Guide]] to plan a visit to Texas Tech.
