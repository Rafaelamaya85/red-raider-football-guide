# The Masked Rider

The Masked Rider is one of the most important traditions at Texas Tech. The rider dressed in all black leads the Red Raiders onto the field while riding a horse before home football games.

The tradition is a major part of the game day experience and represents the spirit and pride of Matador.

## History of the Masked Rider

The Masked Rider tradition officially began in 1954 when Joe Kirk Fulton rode onto the field at the Gator Bowl. The entrance made such an impression that the Masked Rider became an official Texas Tech mascot.

Since then, different Texas Tech students have auditioned and been selected to serve as the Masked Rider and represent the university.

## Game Day Tradition

Nothing compares to the energy pre-game when the Masked Rider leads the football team onto the field in front of the thousands of West Texas Red Raider fans. 

The rider and horse race across the field before the Red Raiders make their entrance. The Tortillas fly, and the Raider faithful show our boys love. It has become a staple that makes attending a football game in Lubbock a unique experience.
