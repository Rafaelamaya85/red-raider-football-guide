# Raider Red

Raider Red is one of the official mascots of Texas Tech University and one of the most recognizable symbols of the Red Raiders. Unlike the Masked Rider, Raider Red is a costumed mascot who can interact with fans at games and other university events.

## History

Raider Red was created in 1971. The character was based on a drawing by cartoonist Dirk West, who was well known for his illustrations of Southwest Conference mascots.

Raider Red gave Texas Tech a mascot that could travel and appear at events where bringing the Masked Rider and horse was not practical.

## Game Day

Raider Red is a major part of the game day experience at Texas Tech. He can often be seen interacting with fans, taking pictures, and helping build excitement during athletic events.

The mascot has become especially popular with younger Red Raider fans.

## Tradition

Raider Red represents the spirit and personality of Texas Tech. Along with the Masked Rider, he has become an important part of the traditions surrounding Texas Tech athletics.
