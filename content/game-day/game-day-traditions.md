# Texas Tech Football Game Day Traditions

Texas Tech football has traditions that make attending a game in Lubbock different from simply watching it on television. The energy from the students, alumni, and fans creates an atmosphere that is a major part of the experience. For someone attending a Texas Tech game, learning about these traditions beforehand can make game day even more enjoyable.

## Traditions Around the Stadium

One of the most exciting parts of Texas Tech is the school spirit surrounding the football program. Fans wear red and black, sometimes white, throughout the town, while the campus fills up with crazed tailgaters pre-gaming. 

Some things fans can expect on game day include:

- Fans wearing Designated colors 
- Tailgating around campus
- The Guns Up hand sign
- The Matador Song
- Tortillas Flying 
- Entrance by the Masked Rider

### The Masked Rider

One of my favorite Texas Tech traditions is the [[masked-rider]]. The Masked Rider represents the university and is one of the most significant traditions at Texas Tech. Seeing the rider and horse in person is something that adds to the atmosphere of attending a football game in Lubbock.
