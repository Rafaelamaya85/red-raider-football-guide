# Where to Eat on Texas Tech Game Day

Food is an important part of any football weekend especially in Lubbock. Whether you want barbecue, burgers, Mexican food, or something quick before the game, there are plenty of options around campus. For me, finding a good place to eat is a crucial part of the entire game day experience.

## Eating Before the Game

On game day, restaurants near campus can become crowded quickly. I would recommend eating earlier than normal or giving yourself extra time before heading toward the stadium. The last thing I would want is to be waiting for a table while kickoff is getting closer.

Some things to consider when choosing a restaurant include:

- Distance from the stadium
- How early the restaurant opens
- Expected game day crowds
- Parking availability
- How much time you have before kickoff

### Planning the Rest of the Day

Where you eat can also depend on your other plans. If you are staying overnight, check out [[where-to-stay]] before deciding where to eat. Fans driving directly to campus should also review the [[parking-guide]] so they know when they need to head toward the stadium.
