# Texas Tech Stadium Entry Guide

Getting to the stadium is the best part, but be ready on what to expect before reaching the gates. A little preparation can make entering the stadium much easier and help avoid unnecessary delays. On a football game day, I would want to arrive early enough to enjoy the atmosphere around campus instead of rushing through the gates right before kickoff.

## Before Heading to the Stadium

Before leaving for the game, fans should make sure they have everything they need like tickets and personal items. I would also check the current stadium rules before leaving because policies can change. Having tickets ready on your phone and knowing where you need to enter can save time once you reach the stadium.

Before heading toward the gates, I would recommend:

1. Have your mobile tickets ready.
2. Check the current stadium bag policy.
3. Know where your seats are located.
4. Determine which entrance you should use.
5. Give yourself extra time to get through the gates.

Getting into the stadium is one of the final steps in the game day plan. Before reaching this point, fans may need to decide [[where-to-eat]], leave their hotel from [[where-to-stay]], and follow their [[parking-guide]].
