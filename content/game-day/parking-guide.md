# Texas Tech Football Parking Guide

Parking is one of the most important things to plan before attending a Texas Tech football game in Lubbock. Because thousands of fans gather and head toward the stadium at the same time, having a parking plan can make game day much easier. I would rather know where I am going before arriving than spend time driving around looking for an open space.

## Planning Before Game Day

Before leaving for the stadium, fans should check available parking options and determine how far they are willing to walk. Some parking areas may require a permit or prepaid parking pass, while other options may be farther away from the stadium. Arriving early is the best course of action because traffic around campus can become hectic as kickoff gets closer.

A few things I would recommend planning ahead include:

- Check parking availability before game day.
- Arrive several hours before kickoff.
- Know the walking distance from your parking area.
- Save your parking location on your phone.
- Expect traffic when leaving after the game.
