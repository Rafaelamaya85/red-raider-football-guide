# Texas Tech Game Day Guide

A Texas Tech football game day in Lubbock is about much more than just the game. From tailgating around campus to the traditions inside the stadium, there is plenty to do before the Red Raiders even take the field.

## Before the Game

Fans begin arriving around campus, hours before kickoff. Tailgating is a major part of the experience, so arriving early gives you time to walk around campus, enjoy the atmosphere, and experience everything happening around the stadium.

## Parking and Getting to the Stadium

Plan ahead for traffic and parking, especially for larger Big 12 games. Lubbock becomes extremely busy on game days, so giving yourself extra time makes it easier to park, walk to the stadium, and enjoy the pregame activities without feeling rushed.

## Traditions to Watch For

One of the biggest traditions is the Masked Rider leading the Red Raiders onto the field before the game. Fans can also experience  Raiderland, Raider Red, and plenty more.

## Inside the Stadium

Try to be inside the stadium well before kickoff. The atmosphere begins building before the game, and you do not want to miss the team entrance or the Masked Rider leading the Red Raiders onto the field.

The student section is also a major part of the atmosphere and helps make Texas Tech home games one of the most exciting experiences in college football.

## My Game Day Tip

My biggest recommendation is simple: **arrive early**. A Texas Tech football weekend is about more than watching the football. Take the time to experience Lubbock, walk around campus, enjoy the tailgates, experience the traditions, and take in the atmosphere at The Jones. This is what it means to truly be a Red Raider. A Matador.
