# Where to Stay for a Texas Tech Football Game

If you are traveling to Lubbock for a Texas Tech football game, choosing where to stay can make a big difference in your weekend. I would prefer to stay somewhere close to Texas Tech so I can spend more time enjoying the game day experience and less time driving across Lubbock. Hotels can also fill up quickly during home football weekends, so planning ahead is important.

## Choosing a Place to Stay

Location is one of the biggest things I would consider when choosing a hotel. Staying close to campus can make getting to the stadium easier, but hotels farther away may offer better prices or availability.

When looking for a place to stay, I would consider:

- Distance from the Texas Tech campus
- Hotel price
- Parking availability
- Distance from restaurants
- Driving time to the stadium
- Check-in and check-out times

### Planning Your Football Weekend

A hotel can serve as the starting point for the entire game day. Before leaving the hotel, I would already have a plan for [[where-to-eat]] and review the [[parking-guide]] so I know where I am going.

Fans should also give themselves plenty of time to get to the stadium. After parking, the [[stadium-entry-guide]] can help with preparing to enter the game.

> Staying close to the action and planning ahead can make a Texas Tech football weekend much easier and more enjoyable.
