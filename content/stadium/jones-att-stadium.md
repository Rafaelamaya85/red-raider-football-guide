# Jones AT&T Stadium

Jones AT&T Stadium is the home of the Texas Tech Red Raiders football team in Lubbock, Texas. Located on the north end of campus, the stadium is the center of the gameday experience for Red Raider football fans.

![[assets/jones-stadium.jpg]]

## History

The stadium opened in 1947 and has gone through several expansions and renovations over the years. These improvements have helped the stadium grow along with the Texas Tech football program.

Beginning with the 2026 season, the stadium was renamed Galaxy Stadium, while the student section carries on the history with the name "The Jones at Galaxy Stadium." But for us diehards , it will always be **The Jones**.


## Game Day at The Jones

On football game days, thousands of fans gather around the stadium and campus before kickoff. Tailgating just outside at Raiderland, the Masked Rider, the Tortillas and the student section all contribute to the atmosphere.

You know its time when the Tortillas fly and the Masked Rider leads the football team onto the field.

## Iconic Moment

Arguably the greatest moment in Texas Tech football history happened at The Jones on November 1, 2008. With Tech trailing No. 1 Texas in the final seconds, Graham Harrell connected with Michael Crabtree for the game winning touchdown with only one second left. The 39–33 victory gave Texas Tech its first win over a consensus No. 1-ranked team and created a moment Red Raider fans will never forget.
