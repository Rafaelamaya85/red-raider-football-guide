# Texas Tech Football Stadium

The home of Texas Tech football is one of the most recognizable places on the Texas Tech campus. Located in Lubbock, the stadium has been the setting for generations of Red Raider football games, traditions, and memorable moments.

## Jones AT&T / Galaxy Stadium

[[jones-att-stadium|Jones AT&T Stadium]], currently renamed Galaxy Stadium, has served as the home of Texas Tech football since 1947. Over the years, the stadium has grown and changed along with the football program while remaining an important part of the Red Raider game day experience.

The stadium has hosted many memorable games in Texas Tech history, including the [[../football-history/2008-texas-game|2008 Texas Game]].
