# Air Raid Offense

The Air Raid offense became an important part of Texas Tech football during the 2000s. Under head coach Mike Leach, the Red Raiders became known for throwing the football frequently and producing some of the most productive passing offenses in college football.

## Mike Leach and the Air Raid

Mike Leach brought his version of the Air Raid offense to Texas Tech when he became head coach in 2000. The offense relied heavily on the passing game and spreading players out across the field to create opportunities for receivers.

This style helped give Texas Tech a unique identity and produced many memorable games during Leach's time in Lubbock.

## Players and Success

Several Texas Tech players put up impressive numbers in the Air Raid offense. Quarterback Graham Harrell and wide receiver Michael Crabtree became two of the best known players from this era.

Their success was especially noticeable during the 2008 season, when Texas Tech started 10-0 and defeated No. 1 Texas in Lubbock.

## Legacy

The Air Raid became closely connected with Texas Tech football and helped influence offensive football beyond Lubbock. Its impact can still be seen in passing offenses throughout college football.
