# 2025 Big 12 Championship Season

The 2025 season became one of the most historic seasons in Texas Tech football history. Under head coach Joey McGuire, the Red Raiders set a school record for wins, captured their first Big 12 football championship, and earned their first appearance in the College Football Playoff.

![Texas Tech celebrating the 2025 Big 12 Championship](../assets/2025-big-12-championship.jpg)

## A Historic Season

Texas Tech finished the 2025 season with a 12-2 overall record. The Red Raiders went 8-1 in Big 12 play and set a new school record for wins in a single season.

One of the things that made the season memorable was how dominant Texas Tech could be on both sides of the ball. The Red Raiders entered the postseason with one of the country's top scoring offenses while also having one of the nation's top defenses.

## Big 12 Champions

The biggest moment came on December 6, 2025, when Texas Tech faced BYU in the Big 12 Championship Game at AT&T Stadium in Arlington.

Texas Tech defeated BYU 34-7 to win its first Big 12 football championship. The victory gave the Red Raiders their 12th win of the season and secured the program's first outright conference championship since its days in the Border Conference.

## College Football Playoff

Winning the Big 12 Championship also sent Texas Tech to the College Football Playoff for the first time in program history.

The Red Raiders received the No. 4 seed and a first round bye. Texas Tech advanced directly to the College Football Playoff Quarterfinal at the Orange Bowl, where the Red Raiders faced Oregon.

Despite losing to Oregon, making the playoff represented a major milestone for a program that had never previously competed for a national championship through the College Football Playoff.

## Legacy

The 2025 season established several new milestones for Texas Tech football. The Red Raiders won a school record 12 games, captured their first Big 12 championship, made their first College Football Playoff appearance, and finished No. 7 in the final AP and Coaches polls, the highest final ranking in program history.
