# Texas Tech Rivalries

Rivalries have played an important role in Texas Tech football history. Over the years, games against other Texas schools have created memorable moments and added excitement to the football season.

## Texas

Texas Tech and the University of Texas have played many memorable games over the years. One of the most famous, and arguably greatest game in school history, came in 2008 when the Red Raiders defeated No. 1 Texas 39-33 in Lubbock.

The game ended with Graham Harrell connecting with Michael Crabtree for the game winning touchdown with only seconds remaining.

## Texas A&M

Texas Tech also has a long history with Texas A&M. The two schools regularly played each other when they were members of the Southwest Conference and later the Big 12.

Although conference changes and realignment ended the annual matchup, games between the two programs remain an important part of Texas Tech football history.

## TCU

Texas Tech and TCU have also shared conference history and competed against each other for many years. The matchup continued when TCU joined the Big 12 and became another important in-state game on Texas Tech's schedule. The upcoming matchup in 2026 carries significant weight as TCU disagreed publicly with how Texas Tech constructed their roster via NIL rules.
