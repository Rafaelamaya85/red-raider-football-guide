## Texas Tech Football History

### About This Category

This section of the Red Raider Football Guide focuses on the history of Texas Tech football. Texas Tech has a long football tradition that dates back to the early years of the university and has grown into an important part of the culture in Lubbock and West Texas.

This category will organize information about important moments throughout the history of the Red Raiders, including memorable seasons, coaches, players, bowl games, and other major accomplishments.
