# 2008 Texas vs. Texas Tech

On November 1, 2008, Texas Tech played the biggest game in program history when the undefeated Red Raiders faced the No. 1 ranked Texas Longhorns at The Jones in Lubbock.

## A Massive Night in Lubbock

Texas Tech entered the game with an 8-0 record and was ranked No. 7 in the country. Texas came to Lubbock undefeated and the consensus   No. 1 ranked team in the country.

The Red Raiders took control early and eventually built a 29-13 lead. Texas fought its way back into the game and took a 33-32 lead late in the fourth quarter.

## Harrell to Crabtree

With only seconds remaining, quarterback Graham Harrell led Texas Tech down the field for one final drive.

With eight seconds left, Harrell threw the ball toward Michael Crabtree near the sideline. Crabtree made the catch, escaped two all conference Texas defenders, and ran into the end zone with only one second remaining.

Texas Tech won the game 39-33.

## A Red Raider Moment I'll Never Forget

The Harrell-to-Crabtree touchdown is one of the most iconic moments in Texas Tech football history. Fans stormed the field as the Red Raiders celebrated their first victory over a No. 1 ranked team.

For Red Raider fans, all you have to say is **"Crabtree pulls free"** and we know exactly where we were that night.
